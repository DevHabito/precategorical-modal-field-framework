# A114-B2-A — corrections, scope, and rejected overclaims

## Promoted result

Within the frozen analytic tail, if the compressed maximizer is strictly `b+2` and

\[
F_{b+2}^{up}>0,
\]

then gamma-plus at contact `b+2` is the unique strict global lifted optimum.

## What is deliberately not promoted

1. `strict b+2 => gamma-plus` — false in general; A114-B2-W1 at `M=521,s=129/1000` is an exact non-gamma-plus counterexample.
2. `Phi<0 => q0/q1 gamma-minus` — not proved and contradicted as a universal finite-atlas architecture label by the presence of gamma-minus, two-band, endpoint-released and q0/q1 subfamilies on the negative-pivot side.
3. `Phi=0` is a strict optimum — false as stated for the gamma-plus candidate because `p_(b+3)=0`.
4. `A102 proves the tail theorem` — false; A102 is finite through `M=520` and is used only as a consistency check.
5. `F_(b+2)^up` alone completely classifies B2` — not proved. It separates the analytically closed gamma-plus positive side from the still-open nonpositive side, but further discriminants may be necessary there.

## Why this theorem is not ad hoc

The proof uses only quantities already present before B2-A was formulated:

- A112-A exact Cramer identities;
- A112-A upper-boundary barriers;
- the independent determinant orientation repaired in A114-B1;
- A113 strict compressed one-variation;
- the already-proved A112 full gamma-plus KKT composition.

No threshold was fitted to A102 and no numerical constant was introduced to separate classes.

\]

A114-B2-W1 proves one exact negative-pivot q0/q1 gamma-minus point but does not identify the general transition law.
