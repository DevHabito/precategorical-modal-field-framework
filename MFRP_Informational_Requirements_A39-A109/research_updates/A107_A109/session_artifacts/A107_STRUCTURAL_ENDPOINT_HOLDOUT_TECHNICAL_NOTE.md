# A107 Structural Endpoint-Sign Holdout — Technical Note

## Question
After the preregistered modular rule was refuted in A107-B3, ask a narrower algebraic question: can the exact sign of the frozen upper-adjacent basic variable `basic_p_{j+1}` at the lower source-segment endpoint predict whether a gamma-plus witness has full source-segment coverage or a proper strict subcomponent?

## Exact algebraic reduction
Only the alpha row depends on the source probe `s`, so the frozen KKT basis matrix has a rank-one row update

\[
A(s)=A_0+e_\alpha v(s)^T.
\]

By the Sherman-Morrison identity, with \(u=A_0^{-1}e_\alpha\),

\[
D(s)=1+v(s)^T u,
\]

and for the basic solution \(x(s)=A(s)^{-1}b\),

\[
x_i(s)=\frac{D(s)x_{0,i}-u_i\,v(s)^T x_0}{D(s)}
      =\frac{N_i(s)}{D(s)}.
\]

The candidate structural variable is therefore

\[
p_{j+1}(s)=\frac{N_{j+1}(s)}{D(s)}.
\]

No floating-point tolerance is used for the predictor; endpoint signs are exact rational signs of the rank-one polynomials.

## Derivation set: canonical ranks 1..25
Before the S1 holdout, the already executed ranks 1..25 showed:

- 25/25 exact classification agreement with the lower-endpoint sign rule;
- 8 partial cases and 17 full cases;
- in every partial case, the only nonpositive KKT condition at the lower source endpoint was `basic_p_{j+1}`;
- all 8 selected partial boundaries were left boundaries generated by `basic_p_{j+1}`.

This was treated only as derivation evidence, not prospective confirmation.

## Frozen predictor
For a record with source interval `(L,R)` and strict source witness `s0`:

1. Require positive common-denominator signs at the lower endpoint, witness, and upper endpoint, and positive `N_{j+1}(s0)`.
2. If `N_{j+1}(L) < 0`, predict `proper_strict_subcomponent` with selected left boundary `basic_p_{j+1}`.
3. If `N_{j+1}(L) > 0`, predict `full_segment_coverage`.
4. If the endpoint sign is zero, or applicability checks fail, make no classification.
5. Do not inspect other KKT conditions, isolate roots, or call the full atlas classifier when generating predictions.

## Interpretation and limits
The B3 modular rule remains refuted. The new evidence supports a different statement: on two independent finite holdouts, the lower-endpoint sign of the exact upper-adjacent basic-variable numerator predicted the full-vs-partial classification without consulting the other KKT conditions or the full root atlas.

This is not yet a theorem for all 922 gamma-plus records. In particular, prospective finite success does not prove that no other KKT condition can become the first boundary later in the catalogue. A rigorous next step should attempt either (i) another unchanged-rule holdout chosen before execution, or preferably (ii) a symbolic sufficient-condition proof that all non-`basic_p_{j+1}` KKT numerators remain positive on the source segment under a precisely stated subclass.
