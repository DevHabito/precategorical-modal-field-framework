## Technical abstract

A78 identified three support sizes at the frozen rational probe

\[
s_0=\frac{131}{1000},
\qquad
\alpha_0=-\log_2 s_0,
\]

for which the strict optimum is not an adjacent four-atom family, but the gamma-inactive compressed branch

\[
P=\{0,k,M\},
\qquad
Q=\{1,h,h+1\},
\qquad
h=\lfloor M/2\rfloor,
\]

with only the \(\alpha+\) and \(\beta-\) bands active. The three pointwise selections were

\[
(M,k)=(40,9),\ (57,12),\ (74,15).
\]

A79 replaces those point certificates by exact connected interval theorems. For each support it isolates two simple algebraic roots

\[
r^-_M<s_0<r^+_M
\]

such that the compressed branch is the strict full-LP KKT basis precisely on the maximal connected component

\[
r^-_M<s<r^+_M
\]

containing \(s_0\). The lower root is where the inactive \(\gamma-\) slack vanishes; the upper root is where the inactive \(\gamma+\) slack vanishes.

The boundary equations have a second exact interpretation. After primitive integer normalization, the lower slack numerator is exactly the Cramer numerator for the atom \(k-1\) entering the adjacent branch

\[
P=\{0,k-1,k,M\},\qquad \gamma-,
\]

and the upper slack numerator is exactly the Cramer numerator for the atom \(k+1\) entering

\[
P=\{0,k,k+1,M\},\qquad \gamma+.
\]

Thus the compression boundaries are simultaneously observation-band activation boundaries and contact-entry boundaries. This is an exact algebraic identity, not a numerical coincidence.

For each support, every KKT condition is strictly positive at \(s_0\). A complete exact root census is then performed on a rational hull enclosing both algebraic endpoints:

- every denominator is root-free;

The census covers 89 conditions at \(M=40\), 123 at \(M=57\), and 157 at \(M=74\). This proves that no hidden basic-variable, dual-multiplier, reduced-cost, inactive-slack, or singularity boundary lies between the two declared roots.

## 3. Why the intervals are maximal

At the lower endpoint,

\[
\operatorname{slack}_{\gamma-}(s)
\]

changes from negative below the root to positive above it. At the upper endpoint,

\[
\operatorname{slack}_{\gamma+}(s)
\]

changes from positive below the root to negative above it.

Therefore the compressed branch cannot be extended through either endpoint while remaining KKT feasible. Since all other KKT expressions are root-free on the enclosing hull, the interval between the roots is the maximal connected strict-KKT component containing \(s_0\).

This is stronger than proving positivity on a convenient rational subinterval. The endpoints are generated by the first actual KKT failures on both sides.

## 4. Exact contact-entry identity

Let \(F^-_{M,k}\) be the adjacent branch

\[
P=\{0,k-1,k,M\},
\qquad
\{\alpha+,\beta-,\gamma-\},
\]

and let \(F^+_{M,k}\) be

\[
P=\{0,k,k+1,M\},
\qquad
\{\alpha+,\beta-,\gamma+\}.
\]

Cramer's rule gives the entering atom as a determinant ratio. A79 compares the primitive monic numerator polynomials exactly and obtains

\[
\operatorname{num}\bigl(\operatorname{slack}_{\gamma-}^{\mathrm{compressed}}\bigr)
=
\operatorname{num}\bigl(p_{k-1}^{F^-_{M,k}}\bigr),
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{num}\bigl(\operatorname{slack}_{\gamma+}^{\mathrm{compressed}}\bigr)
=
\operatorname{num}\bigl(p_{k+1}^{F^+_{M,k}}\bigr),
\]

for each \(M=40,57,74\), after the same primitive monic normalization.

The transition therefore has a genuine pivot interpretation:

\[
\{k-1,k\},\gamma-
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\{k\},\gamma\text{ inactive}
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\{k,k+1\},\gamma+.
\]

The band slack and the entering atom vanish at the same algebraic point because they are governed by the same exact polynomial.

## 6. Evidence classification

A79 is an **exact finite computational certificate**.

The proof objects are:

1. symbolic rational KKT expressions for each compressed branch;
2. exact primitive integer boundary polynomials;
3. rational isolating intervals with multiplicity one;
4. exact root enumeration for every KKT numerator and denominator on the enclosing hull;
5. exact Cramer determinant identities for the entering atoms;
6. exact rational full-KKT witness evaluations outside each interval.

Floating-point decimals are used only for readable summaries. They do not determine any verdict.

## 7. What A79 proves

A79 proves, under the frozen contract:

1. exact maximal connected compression intervals around \(s_0\) for \(M=40,57,74\);
2. simple lower and upper algebraic boundaries for every interval;
3. absence of any other KKT or denominator root between those boundaries;
4. exact sign reversal of the relevant inactive gamma slack at each boundary;
5. exact polynomial identity between each gamma-slack boundary and the corresponding entering-atom Cramer numerator;
6. strict global KKT selection of the expected adjacent branch at an exact rational witness on each outer side.

## 8. What A79 does not prove

A79 does not prove:

1. complete maximal intervals for the adjacent branches;
2. that \(40,57,74\) form a periodic or arithmetic law;
3. an all-\(M\) criterion for when compression occurs;
4. that no other compression supports occur above \(M=80\);
5. a full \(2\le\alpha<3\) phase atlas for these large supports;
6. any physical meaning for support contacts, gamma activity, or active-set compression.

The three compression intervals are not evidence for a physical phase transition. They are exact combinatorial transitions of the declared minimax linear programme.

## 9. Next rigorous target

The next defensible question is not to fit a recurrence to \(40,57,74\). It is to derive a support-size criterion for the existence of a nonempty compression interval.

A suitable A80 target is:

> derive parity-reduced formulas for the two boundary polynomials as functions of \((M,k)\), and determine whether a compression interval exists exactly when the lower root lies below the upper root.

The first gate should be negative-friendly: the formulas may fail to collapse to one simple inequality. A finite family theorem or a proof of irreducible contract dependence would both be scientifically acceptable outcomes.
