## Verdict (reviewer to complete)

Select **one**: valid within its scope / valid subject to correction / unclear / false with counterexample / unable to assess.

Required checks:
1. Is the chosen score well-defined and its logarithm applied to a positive quantity?
2. Is the centered contraction applied to marked **positions**, with weights unchanged?
3. Does the paper distinguish \(\lambda=2\log2\) from the older \(\lambda=\log2\) witness?
4. Are mass, mean and the initial exponential moment exactly equal?
5. Does a strict difference in the transformed exponential moment imply a strict difference in the output score?
6. Is the rank statement restricted to the stated fixed support, not made into a global minimum-support claim?
7. Does the conclusion refrain from ruling out all finite-dimensional or restricted-family closures?
8. Is the comparison with existing moment-closure literature sufficiently precise? Does a stronger prior theorem subsume the result?

For each issue, supply a theorem/equation reference, severity (blocking/major/minor/editorial), an independently checkable calculation or citation, and the preferred correction. A critical review is as useful as a favorable one.

## Mandatory preservation

The repository must retain the date, source commit, original criticism, reproducible counterexample if one is found, change made, and follow-up verdict. Do not mark \`EXTERNALLY_CHECKED\` or \`PEER_REVIEWED\` merely because this form exists, because CI passes, or because an AI agrees with the manuscript.

An initial blind attempt must be submitted **before** the solution is revealed if it is to count as V2 under \`consolidation/INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION_PROTOCOL.md\`. A later, informed specialist critique is still useful V4 evidence but should be labeled separately.
