## Allowed labels

- **EXACT DIAGNOSTIC** — exact calculation in a declared finite physical model.
- **RETROSPECTIVE PHYSICAL CALIBRATION** — mapping checked against a published experiment whose outcome is already known.
- **CLASSICAL STRUCTURE** — established physics or mathematics used as a constraint.
- **EXACT COUNTEREXAMPLE** — explicit construction refuting a stronger mapping.
- **EXACT SUITABILITY OBSTRUCTION** — proof that a proposed experiment cannot test a specified claim.
- **OPEN PHYSICAL HYPOTHESIS** — proposed physical statement without validation.
- **EMPIRICALLY SUPPORTED** — reserved for a prospective or genuinely held-out comparison.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED** — blocks an unsupported inference.

## Promotion rule

No item may be labeled **EMPIRICALLY SUPPORTED** unless all of the following are frozen before the target comparison:

1. physical dataset or published model;
2. map from measured quantities to framework variables;
3. calibration parameters and their provenance;
4. held-out observable;
5. prediction and failure criterion;
6. comparison with at least one established or simpler alternative.

A successful post-hoc fit does not satisfy this rule.
