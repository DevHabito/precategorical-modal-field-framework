## 1. Question

Wang (2019), *Geometry of small causal diamonds*, derives the vacuum Alexandrov interval volume through:

- Eq. (74): the null-cone boundary;
- Eq. (75): the doubled upper-cone volume integral;
- Eq. (76): the Ricci-flat Riemann-normal-coordinate volume density;
- Eqs. (7)--(11): the required spherical Weyl contractions.

The printed final Eq. (79) has numerator

\[
\begin{aligned}
&(7d^3+58d^2+146d+108)E^2\\
&\quad
+6(d+2)(d+6)H^2
-\frac32(d+2)D^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

A direct re-integration of the preceding equations gives different \(E^2\) and \(H^2\) coefficients.

## 8. What this does and does not establish

The all-\(d\) formulas above are not obtained by extrapolating the \(d=4\) result.

They follow from re-integrating the published general-dimensional intermediate equations.

The exact plane-wave calculation independently validates one nontrivial linear combination of \(A_d,B_d,C_d\) for every dimension.

However, Eq. (74) in the source paper is explicitly described as abridged: terms judged irrelevant after angular integration were not printed.

In \(d=4\), an exact plane-wave transformation to RNC confirms that the omitted terms are indeed angularly irrelevant for the volume.

A corresponding exact general-dimensional check of every omitted tensor sector has not yet been completed.

Therefore the appropriate status is

\[
\boxed{
\text{all-d correction implied by the published intermediate equations,}
}
\]

with strong but not complete exact-geometry validation.

## 10. Next target

The next all-dimensional verification should use a second exact Ricci-flat family that is not type N, so that it probes a linear combination of

\[
E^2,\qquad H^2,\qquad D^2
\]

independent of the Cahen--Wallach relation.

A higher-dimensional Kasner family is a natural candidate because:

- it is exactly Ricci flat;
- the central comoving line is geodesic;
- null geodesics retain translation first integrals;
- its Weyl tensor is generically non-radiative.

That calculation can test whether the corrected \(A_d\) and \(C_d\) sectors survive independently beyond four dimensions.
