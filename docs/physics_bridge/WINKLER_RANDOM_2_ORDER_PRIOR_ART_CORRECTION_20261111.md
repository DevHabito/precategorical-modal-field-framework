## 1. Source

Peter Winkler,

> *Random orders of dimension 2*

**Order 7**, 329–339.  
Published December 1990; commonly cited as 1991.  
DOI: **10.1007/BF00383197**.

The accessible Springer abstract states that the paper relates several random models of two-dimensional partial orders and determines limiting probabilities for properties including having a **uniquely orientable comparability graph**.

One of the sample spaces named explicitly in the abstract consists of orders

> generated by pairs of random linear orderings.

The full article was not available through the present access path, so claims below are restricted to what can be established from the bibliographic record and abstract.
